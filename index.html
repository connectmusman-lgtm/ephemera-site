About Sheila

A 50-something woman who loves fashion, art, books and quirky things. Ephemera is a collection of her daily fashionable outfits, and cat pictures.

She has been blogging since 2008, mostly in second-hand clothes, and shops her closet more than the shops. Her partner L, her Mom, and two cats, Barnabas and Whateley, show up often.
